Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of All Notepad Pro, developed by Naveed Ali Chandio.

1. Data Storage

All notes, texts, and data you input into this application are stored 100% locally on your device using native browser storage (LocalStorage). We do not have servers, and we do not upload, read, or share your personal notes.

2. Advertising (AdMob)

To keep this application free, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers and network information to provide relevant ads. No personal notepad data is ever shared with advertising networks.

This app requires internet permission solely for the purpose of loading Google AdMob advertisements. It does not require camera, location, or contact permissions.
