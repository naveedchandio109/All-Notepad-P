Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how All Notepad Pro (developed by Naveed Ali Chandio) handles user information, data security, and advertising compliance.

1. 100% Local Data Storage

All notes, titles, texts, and theme preferences created inside All Notepad Pro are stored locally on your device using secure internal storage (LocalStorage). We do not operate external cloud databases, and we never transmit, read, collect, or share your private notes with any third party.

2. Advertisements & Google AdMob

To keep All Notepad Pro free for all users, we integrate Google AdMob for displaying Banner and Interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personal device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse location, and diagnostic usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google Play policies.

3. App Permissions

The application only requires standard Internet and Network State permissions to load advertisements. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or private files.

4. Children's Privacy & Security

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Users have full control to edit or permanently delete their notes at any time directly within the app.

5. Developer Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the developer, Naveed Ali Chandio, through the official app support listing.
